<modes>
Choose exactly one mode per picture.
ui          card on green. Data and structure: real numbers, comparisons of real amounts, goals with a percentage, lists, choices, X versus Y, formulas, before and after.
material    card on green. Physical things or materials shown realistically with real textures (steel, porcelain, wood, stone, glass).
symbol      card on green. One generic icon or one simple line drawing for something that has no logo (a bell for "distraction", two reaching hands for a famous painting).
scene       FULL FRAME animated illustration. A place, a moment, a person doing something, a story beat, a feeling, or an abstract idea turned into ONE picturable moment or object (a mousetrap baited with a trophy, a person pedalling a bicycle that goes nowhere).
logo        FULL FRAME brand logo. The line names a brand, platform, company, product, app, channel, or a creator who is known by a brand name (YouTube, MrBeast, Skool, Netflix).
person      FULL FRAME picture of a named real public person. You choose how: render "animated" (a drawing in the scene art style) or render "real" (a realistic portrait photograph).
real_image  No drawing. Only when the authentic photo or footage is the point (the actual painting, a real place, a real product demo, a news clip).
</modes>

<mode_decision>
Ask these questions in order and stop at the first yes.
1. Does the line name a brand, platform, company, product, app or channel, and is that name the subject of the line? -> logo.
   - If the same line states real numbers, a list or a formula, the data card wins and the logo is skipped.
   - The same brand does not get a second logo within about 30 seconds.
2. Is the line about a named real public person (their story, behaviour, achievement or lesson)? -> person. Choose the render:
   - the line is really about their channel, brand or company -> use logo instead (question 1);
   - animated (the default): living creators and public figures, anything about behaviour, a story or a lesson, and every historical figure who was never photographed;
   - real: a modern public figure when a believable portrait adds credibility (a founder, an athlete, an author) and a neutral portrait is enough;
   - when the authentic photo or footage of that person is the point -> real_image.
3. Is the authentic real-world picture the point of the line? -> real_image with a search_hint.
4. Does the line state real numbers, amounts, percentages, a comparison of amounts, steps, a list of examples, a formula, a choice between two options, X versus Y, or a before and after? -> ui with the matching layout.
5. Is it about physical materials or objects where the real texture is the message (steel versus ceramic)? -> material.
6. Does one generic icon or one simple line drawing say it better than a scene? -> symbol.
7. Everything else -> scene. This includes feelings, behaviours, stories, historical moments (show the idea behind the event, not a reconstruction), warnings, and abstract ideas such as traps, distractions, myths and "it feels like progress but it is not".
</mode_decision>
