<task>
PLAN. Read the whole timed transcript first, so you understand what the video is about and how its argument develops. Then write, for EVERY numbered segment, what a cutaway picture for that segment would show.
</task>

<input>
The transcript comes from a word-level SRT file, grouped into numbered segments:
  [id] start-end spoken text
"id" is the segment number. "start" and "end" are m:ss.s timestamps in the video. The timing is accurate: trust it. Segments are consecutive pieces of one continuous speech. Read them in order, as a viewer would hear them.
</input>

<what_you_decide>
EVERY numbered segment (line) MUST get an entry. You never skip a line, not even a greeting, a transition, a sign-off, filler, a call to action or an opinion. If a line has nothing obvious to show, describe the simplest fitting stand-in: the place or object the line is about, or a person speaking. You decide:
1. WHAT each B-roll shows.
2. Whether a long line is split into 2 or 3 consecutive B-rolls (only when it has distinct sub-ideas, or lasts more than about 8 seconds).
3. Where each split inside a line starts and ends.
</what_you_decide>

<how_to_decide>
1. Go through the segments one by one, in order, from the first to the last. Do not jump ahead and do not leave any out.
2. Every segment gets at least one B-roll. Never merge two segments into one B-roll: each segment has its own B-roll(s).
3. The first B-roll of a segment starts when the segment starts. Extra B-rolls inside the same segment start at the words where the next sub-idea begins. The last B-roll of a segment ends at the end of that segment's last sentence or clause.
4. Never let one image run across two segments. Avoid flicker: no B-roll shorter than about 1.5 seconds.
5. B-rolls never overlap, and together they cover the WHOLE transcript from the first segment to the last, with no line missing.
6. DESCRIBE what the picture must communicate as plain INFORMATION taken from the spoken words, in ONE sentence of about 5 to 20 words.
   - Keep every number, amount, percentage, date and name exactly as spoken (brands, platforms, channels, creators). The next steps need them.
   - If the line is mainly about a brand, platform, company, product, app, channel or creator, start the description with "Logo of NAME:" (example: "Logo of YouTube: where the video is posted").
   - If the line states real numbers or a comparison, say what is compared and the numbers.
   - For feelings, behaviours, stories and abstract ideas, describe the one picturable moment or object that expresses the idea (example: "A person daydreaming about a trophy instead of working").
   - Say only what the speaker says or directly implies. NO style or aesthetics of any kind: no mood, colour, lighting, camera, art style, and no words like beautiful, dramatic or cinematic. No text or captions.
7. If a segment repeats the previous segment's sentence (a retake), give both the same description.
</how_to_decide>

<output_contract>
Reply with JSON only (no prose, no markdown fences, no code block, no artifact), exactly this shape:
{"broll":[{"start_segment":12,"start_text":"...","end_segment":12,"end_text":"...","description":"..."}]}
- "start_segment": id of the segment where the B-roll starts.
- "start_text": 2 to 8 words copied EXACTLY (same spelling, same order) from that segment: the words at which the image appears.
- "end_segment": id of the segment where it ends (same as start_segment, or a later one).
- "end_text": 1 to 6 words copied EXACTLY from the end segment: the LAST words spoken while the image is still on screen.
- "description": see rule 6.
- Entries are in time order and never overlap. Every segment id you were given must appear as "start_segment" in at least one entry. An empty list is never a valid answer.
</output_contract>
